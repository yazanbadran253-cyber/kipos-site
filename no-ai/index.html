How Kipos works

Kipos does not use AI

Kipos is a plant care record. You add your plants, set how often you water them and log what you do. The app does not send your plants, photos or notes to an AI model.

By Kipos · Published September 27, 2026

Many plant apps start with a photo scan. An AI model guesses the species and writes a care schedule for it. Kipos starts with you. You tell it what you grow and how you look after each plant, and it keeps the record.

What Kipos does

  • You add each plant by name. A photo is optional and stays in that plant's record.
  • You choose how often you water each plant. Kipos reminds you when the next watering is due.
  • You log watering, feeding, pruning and any other care on the day you do it.
  • You keep photos, notes and growth phases together, so you can look back at what happened.

What does Kipos not do?

Kipos has none of these features:

  • Plant identification from a photo
  • Care plans or schedules written by a model
  • A chatbot or AI plant doctor
  • Diagnosis from a picture of a leaf

The app does not call an AI service at all.

Why does Kipos leave AI out?

How often a plant needs water depends on pot size, light, season and drainage. A photo of a leaf does not show those things. You can see them, so Kipos lets you set the interval and keeps a record of what the plant did next.

The watering guide shows how to check a pot before you water it.

Where do my photos go?

Your photos are saved to your Kipos account, so they survive a lost or replaced phone. Kipos shows them in your plant's record. It does not send them to an AI provider, and it does not use them to train AI models.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kipos use AI?

No. Kipos does not identify plants, write care plans or answer questions with an AI model. You add each plant by name and set its watering interval yourself.

Can Kipos identify my plant from a photo?

No. Add the plant by name. You can take a photo for its record, but Kipos does not identify it from the photo.

Are my plant photos used to train AI?

No. Kipos does not send your photos to an AI provider, and it does not use them to train AI models.
