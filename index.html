How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

No spreadsheets, and no trying to remember whether you watered the basil on Tuesday.

  1. 1

    Add what you're growing

    Scan a plant with the camera or add it by hand. Tell Kipos which room or bed it lives in and how much light that spot gets.

  2. 2

    Get a care plan

    Kipos works out a watering, feeding and light schedule for each plant, based on the spot you put it in and the climate where you live.

  3. 3

    Log it in two taps

    Open the app, see what is due today, tick it off. The schedule rolls forward on its own and your streak keeps counting.

What's inside

Everything a garden needs remembering.

Watering log

One watering per plant per day, recorded with the date. Look back and see exactly when each plant last had a drink.

Daily care streak

Clear the day's tasks and the streak grows. Miss a day and there is a little grace built in, because gardens are not exams.

Garden score

One number for how the whole garden is doing, with suggestions for what would move it most. Tap it to see what to fix first.

Photo journal

Photograph a plant whenever you like. The pictures stack up into a record of how it changed over the season.

Shareable cards

Turn a good day, a streak or the whole garden into a card you can post. Built to look right without any editing.

Feeding plans

Feeding is its own schedule, not an afterthought bolted onto watering. Set a cadence per plant and Kipos reminds you when it is due.

Set it up once

Tell it where each plant lives.

A plant on a shaded balcony does not want the same schedule as one on a south-facing sill. Kipos asks the questions that change the answer.

  • The room or spot you keep it in
  • Pots, raised beds, in-ground or windowsill
  • How much light that spot gets
  • When you last watered it, or "I am not sure"

Share your garden

Show off what you're growing.

Every plant in one place, sorted into vegetables, herbs and flowers, with a garden score across the top and a share button that turns it into a card.

Grow the collection

Watch your garden get bigger.

Kipos looks at what you already grow and suggests what would go well next. Each one says why, in a sentence, rather than handing you a list to go and research.

Help

Frequently asked questions.

Which devices does Kipos run on?

iPhone. It is not built for iPad, and there is no released Android version.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Your garden, care history and photos are saved to your account so they survive a new phone. You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password.

When can I download it?

Soon. There is no date to announce yet. When Kipos is out, the button at the top of this page becomes an App Store link.
