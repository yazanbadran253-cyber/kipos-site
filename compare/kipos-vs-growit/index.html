Kipos vs GrowIt

GrowIt is built around planning and caring for an edible garden. Kipos is built around the ongoing record of individual plants and a smaller garden as a whole.

The short answer

Choose GrowIt if bed layout, companion planting, location-based planting dates and produce guidance drive your decision. Consider Kipos if you want each plant's current phase, photos, notes and care record to build into a garden history you can share.

What each app is for

GrowIt's current App Store listing focuses on vegetables, fruit and herbs. It includes a square-foot garden planner, planting calendars based on location, companion-planting information, weather guidance, care reminders and photo-based disease identification.

Kipos's App Store listing starts with plant identification and a care plan. Its ongoing use centers on daily care, plant cards, phases, photos, notes, scores and shareable care cards.

Which one should you choose?

Choose GrowIt when planning comes first

GrowIt is the clearer fit for designing a vegetable bed, checking companion plants, choosing planting dates for your location or following produce-specific guidance through harvest.

Consider Kipos for the record

Kipos fits a balcony, container garden, raised bed or mixed collection where you want to remember the phase of each plant, what you did, and what changed in the photos and notes.

Kipos's current limits

Kipos is available only in English on iPhone. It has no Android release and no visual bed-layout planner. It is also new, with no public rating history yet. GrowIt has broader platform support and deeper tools for planning an edible garden.
